Approach

How we build.

01

API-first

Every product exposes a documented API, so it connects to your CRM, payroll or reporting tools, and to AI agents over MCP.

02

Your data, your call

Data is never shared by default. Access for integrations or third parties is granted explicitly, per organisation.

03

Secure by design

Organisation and user accounts, role-based access and encrypted connections are part of the design from day one.
